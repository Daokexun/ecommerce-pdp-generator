# Contributing to E-Commerce PDP Generator

Thank you for your interest in improving `ecommerce-pdp-generator`!

## How to Contribute
1. **Fork the Repository**: Clone your fork locally.
2. **Create a Feature Branch**: `git checkout -b feature/new-platform-template`
3. **Commit your changes**: Ensure all prompt templates follow structured markdown standards.
4. **Run the Rubric-100 Evaluation**: Test your prompts against `evals/rubric_100_points.json` to ensure composite score $\ge 85$.
5. **Open a Pull Request**: Provide a clear explanation of your improvements and example outputs.

## Areas We're Looking For
- Platform templates (e.g., Mercado Libre, Shopee, Lazada, Etsy).
- Multi-language psychological translation prompts (German, Japanese, Spanish).
- Advanced Midjourney & Flux visual composition formulas.
